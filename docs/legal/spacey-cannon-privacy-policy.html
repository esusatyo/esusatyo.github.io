Privacy Policy for Spacey Cannon

Last updated: 7 August 2026

Spacey Cannon does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

  • No accounts. The app has no sign-in and no user profiles.
  • No data collection. Spacey Cannon does not collect analytics, usage data, advertising identifiers, or any other information about you or your device.
  • No tracking. The app does not track you across apps or websites and contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • No internet connection required. Spacey Cannon works entirely offline. It makes no network requests of any kind.
  • Local storage only. Your high scores, game statistics, saved game progress, and app settings are stored on your device using Apple's standard UserDefaults storage. None of it is personal information, none of it ever leaves your device, and all of it is deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • No third parties. Because nothing is collected or transmitted, nothing is ever shared with third parties.

Possible iCloud sync in a future version

A future version of Spacey Cannon may offer to sync your game data — high scores, statistics, settings, and saved progress — across your own devices using Apple's iCloud.

The current version does not. It contains no iCloud integration and makes no network requests.

If that feature is added:

  • it would cover game data only, never personal information;
  • the data would move between your own devices through your own Apple Account. It would be handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and would not be sent to the developer or to any third party;
  • this policy would be updated, with a new "Last updated" date, before that version ships.

Sharing screenshots and gameplay

You are welcome to capture and share screenshots, video, and recordings of Spacey Cannon anywhere — social media, video platforms, live streams, articles, or reviews — including on monetized channels. No permission or attribution is required.

Children's privacy

Spacey Cannon does not collect any information from anyone, including children under 13, consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds an online feature), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to me@esusatyo.net.
